# xmoq-moqt-client

A MoQ Transport draft 18 client over WebTransport, on [`xmoq-moqt`](../moqt/README.md) and [`xmoq-moqt-io`](../moqt-io/src/lib.rs): with `--mode` it subscribes to a track, publishes one, announces a namespace, subscribes to one, sends FETCH requests or a TRACK_STATUS, and prints what happens. It is the own Rust peer of the [interop runs](../../interop/README.md), which build it (`target/debug/xmoq-moqt-client`) and drive it; nobody watches a broadcast with it (the terminal viewer is [`xmoq-watch`](../watch/README.md)).

Its flags are the doc comments of `Args` and its parts in `src/main.rs`. Every line it prints to stdout is in `src/report.rs`, and the drivers read them, so their format is a contract with `interop/`. A publisher answers a Standalone FETCH for its track with `--answer-fetch`, by `plan_kept` and `answers` of [`xmoq-publisher`](../publisher/README.md), as the gatherer and the transcriber answer theirs, from the newest 1,024 Objects and 4 MiB of payload it keeps (`MAX_KEPT_OBJECTS`, `MAX_KEPT_BYTES` in `src/published.rs`, **provisional**). With the flag it refuses a Joining FETCH with `NOT_SUPPORTED`, a FETCH of another track with `DOES_NOT_EXIST`, and a 33rd FETCH while 32 are answered, or one whose answer would take the payload the answers hold together past 32 MiB (`MAX_BYTES_HELD`), with `EXCESSIVE_LOAD` and a Retry Interval of 1,001 (`plan_fetch` in `src/main.rs`); without it, every FETCH with `NOT_SUPPORTED`. Its streams are as `@xmoq/moqt` has them but for two things. It stops the subscriber's direction of the request stream with `CANCELLED` as it finishes its own, 3 seconds after the fetch stream was written where the subscriber has not ended that direction by then, and at once beyond 64 FETCH requests that wait so; `@xmoq/moqt` stops it 3 seconds after it finished its own. And where its fetch stream cannot be written, it resets the request stream with `INTERNAL_ERROR` (`step` of `answers`), where `@xmoq/moqt` takes the code from its application.

Its tests read [`fixtures/peer-fetch/`](../../fixtures/peer-fetch/README.md), [`published-fetch/`](../../fixtures/published-fetch/README.md) and [`request-answer-wait/`](../../fixtures/request-answer-wait/README.md).
