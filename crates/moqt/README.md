# xmoq-moqt

MoQ Transport draft 18 in Rust: the wire coding of its messages and Objects, and the state of a session, with no I/O. The streams of a connection are [`xmoq-moqt-io`](../moqt-io/src/lib.rs)'s; the client, the relay and the other programs build on the two. The TypeScript implementation is [`@xmoq/moqt`](../../packages/moqt/README.md).

What of the draft it implements, and how it reports a message it does not decode, is at the top of `src/lib.rs`. Where draft 18 is silent or contradicts itself, both implementations do what the [implementation notes](../../docs/moqt-implementation-notes.md) say. The receive limits both sessions share are [`fixtures/limits/limits.tsv`](../../fixtures/limits/README.md), and each constant of this crate has its reason.

Its tests read the bytes of [`fixtures/moqt-18/`](../../fixtures/moqt-18/README.md) (captured from `moqtail`), [`moqt-18-from-libraries/`](../../fixtures/moqt-18-from-libraries/README.md) and [`moqt-18-by-hand/`](../../fixtures/moqt-18-by-hand/README.md), and the tables both implementations agree on: [`peer-subscribe/`](../../fixtures/peer-subscribe/README.md), [`peer-fetch/`](../../fixtures/peer-fetch/README.md), [`joining-request-id/`](../../fixtures/joining-request-id/README.md), [`early-objects/`](../../fixtures/early-objects/README.md), [`immutable-properties/`](../../fixtures/immutable-properties/README.md), [`request-answer-wait/`](../../fixtures/request-answer-wait/README.md), [`retry-interval/`](../../fixtures/retry-interval/README.md), and the Object Properties of [`loc-04/`](../../fixtures/loc-04/README.md).
