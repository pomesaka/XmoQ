# xmoq-moqt

MoQ Transport draft 18 in Rust: the wire coding of its messages and Objects, and the state of a session, with no I/O. The streams of a connection are [`xmoq-moqt-io`](../moqt-io/src/lib.rs)'s; the client, the relay and the other programs build on the two. The TypeScript implementation is [`@xmoq/moqt`](../../packages/moqt/README.md).

What of the draft it implements, and how it reports a message it does not decode, is at the top of `src/lib.rs`. The states of a session and of each request are [`docs/moqt-state-models.md`](../../docs/moqt-state-models.md). Where draft 18 is silent or contradicts itself, both implementations do what the tables of `fixtures/` say, and the comments of the code give the section of the draft; the rulings no test can hold, and the known gaps, are in the [implementation notes](../../docs/moqt-implementation-notes.md). Its tests read the bytes of [`fixtures/moqt-18/`](../../fixtures/moqt-18/README.md), captured from `moqtail`, and the tables of `fixtures/` both implementations agree on.
