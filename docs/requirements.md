# Requirements and status

**Discussed** means explicitly raised in planning; **proposed** means suggested but not confirmed; **decided** means confirmed by the project owner and not yet implemented; **implemented** means the code is in `main` and a recorded run in `docs/experiments/` exercised it; **validation required** means not proven.

This table is where what is done is kept; the [roadmap](roadmap.md) has only what is left, and the runs are in the [experiment records](experiments/README.md).

| Requirement | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Own MoQ Transport implementation in Rust and TypeScript, pinned to draft 18 | Implemented in part: the codec and session in Rust and TypeScript, a client in each and a relay in Rust, run against `moqtail` peers in the interop run. What of the draft is not implemented is in the [implementation notes](moqt-implementation-notes.md) |
| One-to-many live broadcast | Implemented on loopback: a page's video and sound reach many viewers through the relay (`mise run demo`), and one video track reached 1,000 viewers of the Rust client on one machine ([record](experiments/2026-10-24-one-video-track-to-many-viewers-on-linux.md)). Validation required over real links. The goals are in the row "Latency, capacity"; how relays are added is proposed ([design draft](scale-and-meetings.md)) |
| Browser publishing and viewing, with WebCodecs and MoQ/WebTransport | Implemented in Chromium (Chrome, Edge) on desktop, on one machine: H.264 and Opus of WebCodecs over the own MoQ Transport. Which other browsers are supported is undecided ([open decisions](open-questions.md)); validation required on real links |
| Media packaged as LOC, tracks described by an MSF catalog (`draft-ietf-moq-msf-01`), with the FETCH that needs | Implemented: `@xmoq/msf` reads, follows and publishes a catalog track, and `@xmoq/media` sends and plays video in LOC-04 on draft 18 ([protocols](protocols.md), "Streaming format"); the relay keeps the latest Group of a track that has a publisher (`crates/relay-core/src/cache.rs`). What of MSF-01 is not done is in [`packages/msf/README.md`](../packages/msf/README.md) |
| Audio | Implemented in its smallest form: Opus, mono, page to page through the relay, played by the clock of the picture after one buffer (`createPlayout` of `@xmoq/media`). Validation required: nobody judged the sync by eye and ear ([the demo's limits](../demo/README.md#where-it-ends)) |
| Rust relay and Rust CLI for Ghostty (video, audio, chat) | The relay is implemented as the rows above say. The terminal viewer `xmoq-watch` is a first version that plays picture and sound by one clock and joins the room's chat, run on macOS and on Linux with no sound output ([its README](../crates/watch/README.md)). Validation required: a person saw its picture in Ghostty, and nobody is recorded to have heard its sound |
| Chat | Implemented on one machine: the chat goes over MoQ tracks, through a gatherer beside the relay that sets the one order every viewer sees and keeps the lines in SQLite; why is in the [architecture](architecture.md) ("Chat gatherer"), and the gatherer's limits are its flags ([record](experiments/2026-10-24-chat-gatherer.md)). Validation required: nobody has looked at the chat in a browser or a terminal, and the terminal viewer with its two tokens was not run. Undecided: who may get a token |
| Durable Objects for room state | Implemented in Miniflare: the room is `@xmoq/room` (`packages/room`), asked over HTTP for its relay, the namespace of its broadcast, tokens and the seats of a meeting (the forms, [`fixtures/room-api/`](../fixtures/room-api/README.md)); what happens in a room is carried over MoQ ([record](experiments/2026-10-25-the-room-lets-49-into-a-meeting.md)). Validation required: not on Cloudflare, no authentication |
| SNS event integration | Future goal |
| An agent takes part in a broadcast | Implemented on one machine: a CLI (`xmoq-agent`) subscribes to a room and writes what happens in it as lines of JSON where an agent such as Claude reads it, and says the lines of its stdin in the chat ([its README](../crates/agent/README.md)); a transcriber beside the relay (`xmoq-transcriber`) reads the sound with whisper.cpp and publishes the words as a track ([record](experiments/2026-10-25-the-transcriber-reads-a-voice.md)). Validation required: the processor and memory the transcriber takes. What is undecided is in the [open decisions](open-questions.md) |
| Meetings (many to many) | Decided: **meetings of 49 participants at most**, an agent among them as in a broadcast. Implemented on one machine with three participants: participants find each other with SUBSCRIBE_NAMESPACE, the room holds the limit, and each receives the small video of the first who came and the sound of all ([`@xmoq/meeting`](../packages/meeting/README.md), [record](experiments/2026-10-25-a-meeting-of-pages.md)); an agent takes a seat ([record](experiments/2026-10-25-an-agent-takes-a-seat-in-a-rooms-meeting.md)). Validation required: a meeting of 49, the relay's load, the delay from mouth to ear, and a person hearing it. The rest of the [design draft](scale-and-meetings.md) is proposed |
| Reactions | Implemented on one machine: reactions, and how many are in a room, go over tracks of the gatherer of the chat (the forms, [`fixtures/chat-tracks/`](../fixtures/chat-tracks/README.md)); their limits are the gatherer's flags ([record](experiments/2026-10-27-reactions-from-page-to-page.md)). Validation required: a terminal viewer and an agent in the count, and many senders at once |
| Video codec | Decided: H.264 in Annex B format ([protocols](protocols.md)). Profile, level and key frame interval are undecided ([open decisions](open-questions.md)) |
| Latency, capacity | Goals to aim at, not required: **a delay of 500 ms at most**, from a frame being made at the publisher to its being shown at a viewer (to its arriving, where a run has no screen), at the 99th percentile of the viewers' frames; **one relay serves 500 viewers of a broadcast, and relays are added to build a broadcast of 10,000.** Validation required: no run of 10,000, over real links or on more than one machine; how relays are added is the [design draft](scale-and-meetings.md) |
| Authentication, privacy, recording | A token of the room that the relay checks is decided and implemented ([record](experiments/2026-10-11-authorization-tokens-of-a-room-checked-by-the-relay.md)); who may get one is undecided, as are privacy and recording |
| License and contribution policy | Undecided |
