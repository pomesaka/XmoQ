# @xmoq/tracks

The tracks of an endpoint over a session of [`@xmoq/moqt`](../moqt/README.md), whatever they carry: one track kept on one session, asked for again when its subscription ends or is refused, and the tracks a connection serves to its peer. What a track carries (video, sound, a catalog, lines) is the business of the packages above.

| Export | What it is |
|---|---|
| `keepTrackOnSession(options)` | Plays one track on one session for as long as the session lasts: when its subscription ends, or a SUBSCRIBE for it is refused (REQUEST_ERROR), it is asked for again on the same session after `againMs`, or after the Retry Interval of the refusal (transport §10.6.2). Its states are `KeptTrackState` |
| `stayOnSession(options)` | What `keepTrackOnSession` and `keepPlaying` of [`@xmoq/media`](../media/README.md) ask again by: the pauses of `createAskAgain` of `@xmoq/moqt`, with no I/O and no timer of its own |
| `followTrack(connection, options)` | Follows a track of one numbered item a Group (its Object 0, the number its Group ID) for as long as the session lasts: a SUBSCRIBE that waits 30 s for its publisher (RENDEZVOUS_TIMEOUT), an Absolute Joining FETCH for the newest `recent` items, or after a subscription made again for those after the newest had (`catchUp` at the most, and a gap before them), each item handed on once, and the subscription made again after it ends or is refused (`createAskAgain` of `@xmoq/moqt`), or after a GOAWAY on its stream, which cancels it. It ends when the connection closes. The chat's `lines` and a transcript's `speech` are followed by it. Its rule of what to fetch is `followPlan`, which reads [`fixtures/chat-history/`](../../fixtures/chat-history/README.md); it hands an item on once by `SeenNumbers` |
| `publishTrack(options)` | A track this endpoint publishes, as the requests of the peer see it: a `ServedTrack` that takes the SUBSCRIBE and the FETCH for its Full Track Name. One subscription at a time (a second is refused with DUPLICATE_SUBSCRIPTION) or many; the SUBSCRIBE_OK the caller makes (LARGEST_OBJECT); a SUBSCRIBE whose answer cannot be sent refused with INTERNAL_ERROR and reported with `reportError`; a FETCH refused with NOT_SUPPORTED unless the caller answers it; and after `end`, both refused with DOES_NOT_EXIST, and one whose answer was on the way told of the end. What is written, and how long the end waits for it, is the caller's: the video and the sound of [`@xmoq/media`](../media/README.md), the catalog of [`@xmoq/msf`](../msf/README.md), `said` and `reacted` of [`@xmoq/chat`](../chat/README.md) |
| `tellEnd(track)`, `endedByThePeer`, `wholeNumber`, `DEFAULT_END_TIMEOUT_MS` | PUBLISH_DONE (TRACK_ENDED) that never rejects and reports a failure the peer did not cause; whether a failure is the peer's doing; the check of a publisher's times and counts; the time an end waits by default (3000 ms, **provisional**) |
| `ServedTrack`, `serveTracks(connection, tracks)` | A track that answers the SUBSCRIBE and FETCH requests for it, and the `serve` handlers of a connection that hand each request of the peer to the first track that takes it and refuse the rest with DOES_NOT_EXIST. Every `publishTrack` is one |

It is in layer 1 (`scripts/ci/check-layers.sh`), on `@xmoq/moqt` alone. Its tests read the rows of [`fixtures/kept-track/`](../../fixtures/kept-track/README.md) against `keepTrackOnSession` (`keptTrackRules`) and `followTrack` (that README says which rows), and the waits of [`fixtures/followed-track/`](../../fixtures/followed-track/README.md); `xmoq-subscriber` reads both as well.
