# @xmoq/meeting

A participant of a meeting in a page. Every participant publishes a broadcast of its own under `xmoq/meet/<room>/<id>`, and finds the others with one SUBSCRIBE_NAMESPACE for `xmoq/meet/<room>` (decided on 2026-10-25, [design](../../docs/scale-and-meetings.md)). This package receives: it reads each one's catalog, and receives the sound of all of them and the small video of the first 8 who came. Publishing the page's own broadcast is done with [`@xmoq/media`](../media/README.md) and [`@xmoq/msf`](../msf/README.md), as any broadcast is, and playing what is received is the caller's.

| Export | What it is |
|---|---|
| `createMeeting(options)` | A meeting of `room` as `self`, receiving the video of the first `mostWatched` and keeping `mostOthers` others, both the application's to say (the demo: 8, and the seats of its room less one). `attend(connection, signal, onTaken)` takes part on one session until it ends (`onTaken` once its SUBSCRIBE_NAMESPACE was taken); called again with the next session, it keeps who was there. Each decoded frame and piece of sound is handed to `onFrame(id, …)` and `onAudio(id, …)`, and the callee closes it. `onEvent` is told who came and left, and which tracks began and stopped |
| `meetingPrefix`, `participantOf`, `MEET` | The names, as `crates/viewer/src/meeting.rs` has them |
| `createPresence`, `SETTLE_MS`, `watchedOf` | Who is there in the order they came, across sessions, as `Presence` of `crates/viewer/src/presence.rs` has it; and whose video is received |
| `chooseTracks` | The video with the fewest pixels, and the first sound, of a catalog's tracks in its own namespace |
| `keepAttending(options)` | `attend` across sessions: `connect` is called anew for each session (the demo asks the room for its seat and tokens each time), and the next `connect` is handed the GOAWAY that ended a session, so that the caller connects to its New Session URI; the pause between sessions and what a GOAWAY (§10.4) does are `keepMoving`'s ([`@xmoq/moqt`](../moqt/README.md), "Asking again"). The demo's meeting page (`/meet`) attends by it |
| `createAgentBridge(options)`, `openAgentFolder`, `rememberedRoot`, `chooseRoot`, `allowRoot` | A page as a reader for an agent on the same machine (Claude Code): what happens in the meeting is written into `<root>/<room>/<id>/`, laid out as `xmoq-agent --out` lays its own (`events.jsonl`, `inbox.txt`, `frames/<when the writing began>/`), with the lines of [`fixtures/agent-lines/`](../../fixtures/agent-lines/README.md) (`participant`, `chat`, `speech`, `frame` with `id`, `error`; `agentLine`). A line an agent writes into `inbox.txt` is read as a line of stdin of the CLI (`readInboxLine`): said in the chat, or `/frame <id>` for a PNG of the latest picture of `id` (`/frame` alone: whoever spoke last). The root is one folder the person chooses once (the demo asks for `~/Documents/xmoq`), remembered in IndexedDB; a later page asks only to be allowed to write there again |

What it does, in order:

- NAMESPACE of `xmoq/meet/<room>/<id>` (§6.1): `id` came, unless it is `self`, or 48 others are there already (`too-many`). Its catalog is followed, with a RENDEZVOUS_TIMEOUT of 10 s, since a participant announces before its catalog is published; a catalog that ends or fails is followed again after 1 s, or after the Retry Interval of a refusal (`createAskAgain` of `@xmoq/moqt`).
- With a catalog: the sound of everybody is subscribed to, and the smallest video of the first `mostWatched` who are there, in the order they came. A subscription that ends while its participant is there is made again (`keepTrackOnSession` of `@xmoq/tracks`). A catalog that changes the chosen tracks has them subscribed anew.
- NAMESPACE_DONE: the participant left. Its subscriptions are cancelled, and its place among the watched goes to the next who came.
- A new session: whoever was there is followed on it at once, and those the relay does not tell of again within `SETTLE_MS` (2 s) have left.

**Provisional values**: the 10 s of the catalog's RENDEZVOUS_TIMEOUT, as `@xmoq/transcript` has it, the pauses of `createAskAgain`, and `SETTLE_MS`. A sound track without `samplerate` is taken for 48,000 Hz, and one without `channelConfig` for one channel.

Layer 3 (`scripts/ci/check-layers.sh`): it depends on `@xmoq/moqt`, `@xmoq/msf` and `@xmoq/media`. The core (`src/core`) does no input or output.

## Writing for an agent: what to know

- **Chromium alone**: the File System Access API (`showDirectoryPicker`, the permissions of a handle) is in no other browser. Elsewhere the page says so and writes nothing.
- **The folder is chosen once**: the handle is kept in IndexedDB, and a page that comes later asks only to be allowed to write in it again, with a click. Chromium lets a page have no home, desktop or Documents folder itself, only a folder in them; hence `~/Documents/xmoq`.
- **A change of a file is not told**: the page reads `inbox.txt` again every second (`INBOX_EVERY_MS`, provisional), so a line an agent writes is done up to a second later. What is in it when the page begins is passed over. An agent appends to it (`>>`); a file written anew (`>`) is read from its beginning.
- **`events.jsonl` is written once a second, and is another file after each write**: Chromium writes a copy and moves it over the file (the WHY at `flush` in `src/web/agent-folder.ts`), so an agent follows it by its name, `tail -F`, and not `tail -f`. Each write copies the whole file, which grows with the meeting.
- **The person and the agent share one seat**: what the agent says is said under the page's name in the chat, and its pictures are of what the page receives (the video of 8 at most). **This is an experiment, and stays so** (decided on 2026-10-26): the lines and words of the others are kept on the person's disk, and nobody else in the meeting is told; the agent's lines go into the chat under the person's name, and the others cannot tell them from the person's.
- **Closing the page ends the writing**: what was written stays in the folder.
